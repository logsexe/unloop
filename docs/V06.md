# UNLOOP v0.6

V0.6 adds recommendation memory, explicit-feedback-aware ranking, finite-batch analytics, and Spotify quota-aware error handling.

## Product rules

- A recommendation is remembered even if it was never played.
- Explicit likes/dislikes/skips are stronger signals than passive engagement.
- Disliked artists and rejected tracks are excluded from future batches.
- Liked artists receive only a small affinity boost; saturation and novelty still matter.
- Every batch is finite and measurable.
- Spotify quota exhaustion is distinguished from ordinary rate limiting.

## New endpoints

- `GET /v1/analytics/batches`
- `GET /v1/recommendation-memory`

Feedback submitted through `POST /v1/feedback` now updates the outcome of the latest matching recommendation.
