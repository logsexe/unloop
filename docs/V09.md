# V0.9 — Product UI

V0.9 moves UNLOOP closer to the eventual mobile product while preserving the finite-discovery philosophy.

## Added

- Album artwork in discovery results.
- Direct track handoff to the connected streaming service.
- In-app Like, Skip, Nope, and 90-day artist cooldown controls.
- Taste dashboard with taste clusters and artist saturation.
- Batch analytics dashboard with novelty and hit-rate visibility.
- Visible source/explanation details for each recommendation.
- Mobile-responsive finite batch layout.
- GitHub-first development workflow.
- Clear Spotify connected/not-connected status with a disabled connected state.
- Optional custom playlist names with `UNLOOP Discovery` as the default.
- Language, relatability, release-year, genre, and explicit-content controls.
- Provider-neutral service registry, with Spotify as the first adapter rather than the product boundary.

## Product rule

UNLOOP remains deliberately finite: no swipe discovery, no infinite feed, no autoplay recommendation chain.
